# Stakeholder Summary - Sales & Profitability (Sep 2013 - Dec 2014)

**To:** Leadership team  **Re:** Where we make money, where we lose it, and what to do next

## The headline
We booked **$118.7M net sales** and **$16.9M profit (14.2% margin)**. Profit is healthy overall, but two issues are quietly costing us money: **Enterprise pricing** and **discount depth**.

## What the data shows
1. **Government is our engine** - 44% of sales and 67% of profit (21.7% margin).
2. **Enterprise loses money** - $19.6M of sales but a $0.61M net loss. All 58 loss-making deals in the dataset are Enterprise deals.
3. **Why:** Enterprise sells at 125/unit against a 120/unit cost - only a 4% gross margin. Any discount above 4% makes the deal unprofitable.
4. **Discounts erode margin everywhere** - 21.9% with no discount, 17.9% Low, 14.4% Medium, **9.1% High**. High-discount deals are 35% of all deals.
5. **USA** has the most sales but the lowest margin (12.0%) and the highest discount rate (8.2%); France and Germany earn 15.5-15.7%.
6. **Channel Partners and Midmarket** have excellent margins (73% / 28%) but together are under 4% of sales.
7. **Seasonality:** October and December are our biggest months; like-for-like Sep-Dec sales grew **+37%** (2014 vs 2013).

## Recommended actions
| Priority | Action | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Cap Enterprise discounts near 4% or raise the 125 list price | Stops all current loss-making deals (~$0.78M lost) |
| 2 | Introduce a discount-approval policy (start with Enterprise and Small Business) | High-band margin is half the no-discount margin |
| 3 | Grow Channel Partners and Midmarket | Highest margins, smallest share |
| 4 | Review USA discounting vs France/Germany | Largest market, weakest margin |
| 5 | Plan inventory/staffing for Oct and Dec peaks | Two biggest months of the year |

## Caveats
Only 16 months of data (2013 has Sep-Dec only), so growth figures are like-for-like Sep-Dec. The data has no customer-level detail, so the reason behind Enterprise pricing should be confirmed with the sales team.
